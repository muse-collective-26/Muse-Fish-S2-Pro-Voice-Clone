# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-16)
- Muse Fish S2 Prompter: a model list. Press **↻ Models** (or change the provider) and pick a model from the list - Ollama and LM Studio show the models on your computer; ChatGPT and Gemini show their chat models (needs the key).
- New workflow: **Muse Fish 2 Voice Clone**, now in the `workflows/` folder (replaces the old example workflow).
- The workflow has a **Read me** note with links to the node packs and the models.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-16)
- Muse Fish S2 Prompter: adds Fish S2 Pro emotion and delivery tags to your script (keeps your words), or writes a short script. Ollama, LM Studio, ChatGPT, Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible server. Keys only from the environment.
- Muse Whisper Transcribe: the words in your voice sample, for Fish S2's reference_text. Works with current transformers (fixes "Input type (float) and bias type (Half) should be the same").
- Example workflow: Muse Fish S2 Pro Voice Clone.
